### Financial Statements

PeopleProsper Technologies Private Limited experienced a decline in net profit in FY2023 compared to FY2022, primarily driven by increased expenses, particularly in employee benefits and other operating costs. While revenue saw a modest increase, the higher cost structure led to a significant rise in the company's loss for the year.

### Revenue and Profitability

*   **Revenue Growth:** Total revenue increased from INR 1,02,872 hundred in FY2022 to INR 1,91,256 hundred in FY2023. This represents a substantial growth of approximately 86%.
*   **Revenue Composition:** The primary revenue stream is "Revenue from operations," which grew from INR 1,02,011 hundred in FY2022 to INR 1,87,256 hundred in FY2023. "Other Income" also contributed, albeit a smaller amount, increasing from INR 862 hundred to INR 3,956 hundred.
*   **Profitability Decline:** Despite revenue growth, the company's profit (loss) for the year significantly worsened. In FY2022, the company reported a loss of INR 4,06,196 hundred. This loss widened to INR 3,78,339 hundred in FY2023. This indicates a negative trend in profitability despite top-line growth.
*   **Net Profit/(Loss) per Share:** Both basic and diluted earnings per share show a loss, decreasing from (INR 3,589.89) in FY2022 to (INR 3,418.78) in FY2023.

### Expenses Analysis

*   **Overall Expense Increase:** Total expenses rose from INR 4,95,195 hundred in FY2022 to INR 5,69,551 hundred in FY2023, an increase of approximately 15%.
*   **Employee Benefit Expense:** This is the largest expense category and saw a significant increase from INR 2,42,470 hundred in FY2022 to INR 2,67,814 hundred in FY2023, an increase of about 10%. This includes salaries, wages, other incentives, contribution to provident fund, gratuity expense, and staff welfare expenses. The rise in gratuity expense and staff welfare suggests potential increases in headcount or benefit payouts.
*   **Depreciation and Amortization Expense:** This expense decreased from INR 53,796 hundred in FY2022 to INR 48,544 hundred in FY2023.
*   **Other Expenses:** This category saw a substantial increase from INR 1,98,930 hundred in FY2022 to INR 2,51,633 hundred in FY2023, an increase of approximately 26%. This broad category includes advertising, bank charges, dues and subscriptions, insurance, IT and internet charges, office expenses, professional fees, purchase of gift cards, rent, travel, and miscellaneous expenses. The significant jump here is a key driver of the widened loss.

### Cash Flow

*   **Operating Activities:** The company's net cash from operating activities was negative in both years, with an outflow of INR 2,13,587 hundred in FY2022 and INR 3,70,271 hundred in FY2023. This deterioration highlights increasing cash burn from core operations.
*   **Investing Activities:** Net cash used in investing activities was minimal, with an outflow of INR 3,075 hundred in FY2023 compared to an inflow of INR 191 hundred in FY2022.
*   **Financing Activities:** The company generated significant cash from financing activities. In FY2023, net cash from financing activities was INR 4,64,864 hundred, primarily from proceeds from preference shares (INR 4,18,618 hundred) and net proceeds from loan taken (INR 47,318 hundred). This contrasts with FY2022, where financing activities provided INR 3,33,108 hundred.
*   **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** The closing balance of cash and cash equivalents increased from INR 2,00,564 hundred in FY2022 to INR 2,92,079 hundred in FY2023. This increase is largely due to the significant cash infusion from financing activities, which more than offset the operating cash burn.
