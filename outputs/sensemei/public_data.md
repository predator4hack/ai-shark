### Core Products/Services

SENSEMEI primarily offers a portfolio of integrated circuits (ICs) designed for power management and control applications. These are categorized into specific product lines:

*   **Battery Management System (BMS) ICs:**
    *   **Multi-Cell Series Battery Monitoring:** ICs designed for monitoring the voltage, current, and temperature of multi-cell battery packs (e.g., SM2286, SM2288, SM2289 series supporting up to 16-series cells). These typically include features like cell balancing, overcharge/discharge protection, and communication interfaces.
    *   **Battery Charge Management:** ICs for controlling the charging process of batteries (e.g., SM5001, SM5002, SM5004, SM5005 series). These likely implement various charging algorithms (e.g., CC/CV) and protection features.
    *   **Battery Protection:** Dedicated ICs for ensuring the safety of battery packs by protecting against conditions like over-voltage, under-voltage, over-current, and short circuits (e.g., SM380X series).
*   **AC/DC & DC/DC Conversion ICs:**
    *   **DC/DC Converters:** ICs for efficient voltage step-up (boost), step-down (buck), or buck-boost conversion (e.g., SM400X series, SM401X series). These are crucial for power efficiency in battery-powered devices and various power supplies.
    *   **AC/DC Converters:** ICs designed to convert alternating current (AC) to direct current (DC) for power adapters and general power supply applications (e.g., SM86XX series, SM850X series, SM855X series).
*   **Motor Driver ICs:**
    *   ICs specifically designed to control various types of electric motors (e.g., SM70XX series). These typically offer precise control, efficiency, and protection features for motor applications.
*   **LED Driver ICs:**
    *   ICs for controlling Light Emitting Diodes (LEDs) in various lighting applications (e.g., SM75XX series). These often provide constant current or voltage output, dimming capabilities, and power factor correction.

### Key Features & Capabilities

SENSEMEI highlights several technical features and capabilities across its product lines:

*   **High Integration:** Many ICs integrate multiple functions (e.g., monitoring, balancing, and protection in BMS; various control loops in converters) to reduce external component count and simplify design.
*   **High Accuracy:** Especially emphasized in BMS products for precise voltage, current, and temperature monitoring.
*   **High Efficiency:** A core capability for their AC/DC and DC/DC converters, crucial for energy saving and extending battery life.
*   **Comprehensive Protection Features:** Over-voltage (OVP), under-voltage (UVP), over-current (OCP), short-circuit protection (SCP), and over-temperature protection (OTP) are common across their power management ICs.
*   **Low Quiescent Current/Low Standby Power:** Important for battery-powered devices to maximize standby time and for energy-efficient power supplies.
*   **Flexible Communication Interfaces:** BMS ICs often support standard interfaces like UART, I2C, and SPI for communication with microcontrollers.
*   **Wide Input/Output Voltage Ranges:** Applicable to converters, allowing for versatility across different power supply requirements.
*   **Advanced Control Modes:** Mention of various control techniques like PWM (Pulse Width Modulation), PFM (Pulse Frequency Modulation), BCM (Boundary Conduction Mode), and Constant Current/Constant Voltage (CC/CV) control.
*   **Dimming Capabilities:** For LED drivers, enabling brightness adjustment.
*   **Power Factor Correction (PFC):** For AC/DC LED drivers, contributing to higher power quality.
